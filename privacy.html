Vault Sync Privacy Policy

Vault Sync is a private tool used only by the Swink family to sync their own notes in Google Drive.

It reads and writes files only in the family's own Google Drive. It does not collect, sell or share any data with anyone, and it has no other users.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: swink.admin@gmail.com
